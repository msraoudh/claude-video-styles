# Edit plan v1: "Creativity + Intelligence" (reel-ref-edit)

Clean cut v2 runs 1:47. Times are mic seconds (line labels in Studio).
Style: ref1 (frazix) language in the user's brand.
- **Colours:** black #0B0B0B, deep Tunisian red #C8102E / #7A0C17, red→black gradients, off-white #F4EDE4.
- **Fonts:**
  - **Anton:** giant words behind the head (matte).
  - **Inter ExtraBold:** captions.
  - **Caveat:** handwritten accents, red with glow.
  - **Cairo:** Arabic.
- **Rules:**
  - One idea = one visual, entering on the word.
  - Text goes behind the head or under the mic, never on the face.
  - At most one full-screen "dark paper" page every 4–6 s, ≤2.5 s.
  - Punch-in wide↔close every 2–3 s.
  - Quiet SFX, never on a word.

## Captions
- 1–3 words, Inter ExtraBold, white, at chest height under the mic.
- The stressed word, or the second half of the group, in red Caveat handwriting with a red glow.
- Derja is written in Arabic letters, French/English tech words in Latin (as in the split-reel captions).

## Framing (from his 4K)
- Wide = full frame.
- Close = 1.35× punch-in centred on the face, which stays sharp because the source is 4K.
- Alternate on each new sentence (≈ every 2–3 s), and always punch in on the giant-word slams.

## Sound
- Music: a free dark, minimal beat (Mixkit/Pixabay), ducked ~19 dB under the voice, with a drop at line 48. "Shot" (Meechie Alexander) only through Instagram's picker.
- SFX from `sfx\` as listed above, capped 8 dB under the voice and never on a word.
- Master to −14 LUFS.

## Build
- Face matte for behind-the-head words: RVM via reel-pro-edit `matte.py` on the 4K cut.
- Scenes are built in Remotion, one file per section, then checked frame by frame before Studio.
